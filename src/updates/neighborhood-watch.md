# Neighborhood Watch
Neighborhood Watch is an effective way to keep our neighborhood safe and reduce/prevent crime.
As we work together, we need to get to know our neighbors and watch out for each other.
Be informed of and aware of crime in our area by checking out the Boise PD
interactive public [crime map](https://police-transparency-boise.hub.arcgis.com/).

To reduce and prevent residential crime, be sure to secure your home, get to know your
neighbors and work together, and be aware by watching out for each other.
If you notice suspicious activity, note the information such as vehicle
description, plate number and persons involved.
Report criminal and suspicious activity to the PD dispatch
non-emergency number (208-377-6790) and then to our Neighborhood
chairman via [email](mailto:BPHOA.Boise@gmail.com).
